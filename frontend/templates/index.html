General Appeal System

A governance-enforced, multi-actor workflow with strict role separation, immutable decisions, full audit trails, and automatic routing.

Governance Design: Process Flow & Principles

SUBMITTED → UNDER REVIEW → DECIDED → APPEALED → FINALIZED
⚠️ Role Separation Enforced: On appeal, Authority is completely bypassed — the case routes automatically and directly to the independent Reviewer.
